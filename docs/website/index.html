What AgriPulse is

A record of the field, and an action at the end of it.

You map your farms and blocks, connect them to satellite and weather data, and the platform computes indices for every block and every grid cell on each pass.

Decision trees read those numbers together with the weather, what your scouts recorded, the crop stage and how the block was planted. When a rule matches, a recommendation or an alert opens on that block, on that date, with the reason attached. Your team assigns it, sends it to a worker or a scout, and closes it with an outcome.

The platform runs in English and Arabic, and ships with the agronomy already written.

01

Observe

Satellite optical and thermal imagery, daily weather, and what your scouts record in the field, all keyed to the same farm and block shapes.

02

Interpret

Vegetation, water and heat indices per block and per grid cell, compared against the baseline for that block at that time of year.

03

Act

Decision trees open recommendations and alerts. Your team assigns them, dispatches them to a worker or a scout, and closes them with an outcome.

The loop, in five steps

  1. Subscribe. A farm is connected to an imagery provider and to weather. Blocks inherit it.
  2. Acquire. Passes are found and fetched. Weather is pulled every day, history and forecast.
  3. Compute. Indices per pass, per block and per grid cell. Weather signals per day.
  4. Evaluate. Decision trees read the numbers, the crop stage and the field signals, and record why.
  5. Act. Recommendations and alerts open, get assigned, get dispatched, and get closed.

Each step is a process on this site. Start at the top if the farm is new, or jump to the step you are stuck on.
