Onion and garlic

Onion and garlic are in the catalog as long-season vegetables. Garlic carries the longest growing-days figure in the whole catalog.

Catalog only

How to watch them today

  • NDVI against the block baseline through bulking.
  • The rainfall against evapotranspiration balance, because both crops are irrigation-sensitive late in the season.

What is not seeded yet

No stage model, no plan template and no rule. The long season and the low heat limits make these good candidates for a growing degree day rule.
