Create a farm

A farm is the outer boundary of everything you manage in one place. Imagery and weather subscriptions attach to the farm, not to the blocks inside it, so this is the first record you create and the one everything else hangs off.

Who does this

Tenant admin, farm manager

How long it takes

5 minutes, once you know where the boundary runs

Steps

6 steps

Before you start

  • A tenant account and a sign-in that can create farms.
  • The boundary: either you know where it runs on the map, or you have a KML, GeoJSON or zipped Shapefile of it.
  • A code and a name your team already uses for this farm.

Step by step

Create a farm

Open the new farm flow

  • On a new account the workspace opens on No farms yet. Click Create your first farm.
  • If the account already has farms, go to Farm management and start a new farm from there. Both routes open the same two-screen flow at /labs/map?create=farm.
  • Until a farm exists, Insights, Plan, Signals, Action Center, Recommendations, Alerts and Reports are greyed out. They all need a farm to point at.

Step 1 of 2 — draw or upload the boundary

  • Draw boundary lets you click each corner of the farm on the map. Click once per corner, then double-click the last corner to close the shape.
  • Upload file takes a KML, a GeoJSON, or a Shapefile in a zip. Use this when a surveyor already gave you the boundary.
  • Draw the outer boundary only. Blocks come later, inside it.
  • Include the roads, buildings and canals that sit inside the farm. You mark them as land units afterwards so they stop counting towards crop numbers.
Note

Zoom in before you start drawing. A boundary traced at low zoom is off by tens of metres, and every index number for this farm is computed inside that line.

Check the area before you go on

  • As soon as the shape closes, the panel header shows the area, for example 185.5 feddan. The unit follows the tenant unit setting: feddan, acre or hectare.
  • Compare that figure with what you know the farm to be. A number that is far out means the shape is wrong, and it is much cheaper to fix now.
  • Boundary takes you back to redraw. Cancel, at the top right, throws the whole thing away.

Fill the two required fields

  • Code is the short identifier your team uses, for example GV-01. It appears in lists, exports and reports.
  • Name is the readable name, for example Green Valley North.
  • Create farm stays disabled until both Code and Name are filled. Nothing else on the form is required.
  • Set Country even though it is optional. Decision trees can be written for a region, and a farm with no country matches none of them.

Open More details if you have the answers

  • Everything under More details is optional. Skip it if you are recording the farm quickly, and come back later.
  • Primary water source and Farm type are worth setting now. They describe the farm in reports and give context to anyone reading it later.
  • Tags are free text. Use them if you group farms by region or by client.

Create the farm

  • Click Create farm.
  • The farm appears in the farm picker at the top of the workspace, and the workspace pages stop being greyed out.
  • Everything except the boundary can be changed later in Farm settings.
Check it worked
  • The farm shows in the farm picker at the top of the workspace.
  • The area in Farm settings matches what you know the farm to be.
  • The workspace pages are no longer greyed out.

Reference

Every field on the form

FieldRequiredWhat it is for
BoundaryyesThe outer edge of the farm. Drawn on the map, or uploaded as KML, GeoJSON or zipped Shapefile.
CodeyesShort identifier, for example GV-01. Shows in lists, exports and reports.
NameyesReadable name, for example Green Valley North. Shows in the farm picker.
CountrynoOne of 18 countries. Decision trees can target a region, so a farm with no country matches none of them.
DescriptionnoFree text about the farm.
Governorate, DistrictnoAdministrative location.
Nearest city, AddressnoWhere the farm is, in words.
Farm typenoCommercial, Research or Contract. Starts on Commercial.
OwnershipnoOwned, Leased, Partnership or Other.
Primary water sourcenoWell, Canal, Nile, Desalinated, Rainfed or Mixed.
Established datenoWhen the farm started.
TagsnoFree text labels for grouping farms.

When it does not work

What you seeWhat to do
Create farm is greyed outCode or Name is empty. Both are required. Everything else is optional.
The area looks wrongThe shape is wrong, not the calculation. Click Boundary and redraw at a higher zoom.
The boundary cuts through a fieldRedraw before you create blocks. Blocks and grid cells are all computed inside this line.
No country setThe farm still works, but a decision tree written for a region will skip it. Set it in Farm settings.
The upload is rejectedThe file must be KML, GeoJSON, or a Shapefile inside a zip. A bare .shp will not do, a Shapefile needs its sidecar files.
