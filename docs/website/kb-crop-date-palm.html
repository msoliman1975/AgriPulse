Date palm

Date palm has the fullest stage model in the platform, running from dormancy through the four fruit stages known in the field as kimri, khalal, rutab and tamar. Rain during pollination and during ripening are both covered by their own rule.

Growth stages

Calendar based, by day of year: dormancy, pollination, fruit_set, kimri, khalal, rutab, tamar. The names are the ones used in the field, not translations.
