Crop

Potato

Potato is the annual crop with the most depth. Its stage model counts days from planting rather than the calendar, and three of its rules read laboratory petiole results imported from a CSV file.

Growth stages

Counted in days from planting: emergence, tuber_initiation, tuber_bulking, maturation. Because it counts from your planting date, two blocks planted a month apart are read correctly at the same time.

Plan template activities

The potato season templates carry soil preparation, planting, irrigation, fertilizing, hilling, spraying, observation and harvesting.

Attributes recorded at planting

  • seed_material and planting_material
  • seed_tuber_grade, seed_generation, seed_lot_code
  • sprouting_status and seed_treatment
