Mango

Mango is the crop AgriPulse knows best. It has a stage model, six cultivar plan templates, and six decision trees covering disease pressure, canopy health, stress induction and post-harvest nutrition.

Growth stages

Calendar based, by day of year: pre_flowering, flowering, fruit_set, fruit_development, maturation, post_harvest_flush. A decision tree can read the stage directly, so a rule can say at flowering rather than in March.

Plan template activities

The mango templates carry irrigation, fertilizing, pruning, growth regulator, harvesting and observation activities, anchored to the stage they belong to.
