Section B

What AgriPulse is

In one paragraph Outline

Placeholder. AgriPulse is a multi-tenant farm intelligence platform. A tenant maps its farms and blocks, subscribes them to satellite and weather data, and the platform computes indices per block and per grid cell on every pass. Decision trees read those indices together with weather, scouting signals, crop stage and block attributes, and open recommendations and alerts that a team assigns, dispatches and closes.

In one page Not started

Longer version for evaluators. Covers the tenant model, the farm and block model, the imagery pipeline, the weather pipeline, the rule engine, the action loop, the mobile scouting app, Arabic support, and where the data is hosted.

Diagram slot. One system diagram: sources on the left, the platform in the middle, the four output surfaces on the right. To be drawn.

Data lifecycle in five steps Outline

1 · Subscribe

A farm is subscribed to an imagery provider and to weather. Blocks inherit.

2 · Acquire

Passes are discovered, fetched and stored. Weather is pulled daily, history and forecast.

3 · Compute

Indices per pass, per block and per grid cell. Weather derived signals per day.

4 · Evaluate

Decision trees read the aggregates, weather, signals and crop stage, and record a trace.

5 · Act

Recommendations and alerts open, get assigned, dispatched, and closed with an outcome.

Throughout · Watch

Observer tracks acquisition and computation health across tenants.

Crop custom attributes Outline

Per-crop fields a block can record, seeded so every tenant collects the same things. Examples: establishment method, transplant date, age at transplant, rootstock type, nursery source, graft union height, offshoot age and weight, mother palm reference, tissue-culture lab and batch, seed tuber grade, seed generation, seed lot code, sprouting status, seed treatment, seedling age, true leaves at transplant, tray cell count. Decision trees can read these as a condition source.

Build plan for this site

  1. Agree this structure and the section titles.
  2. Write Section A and Section B first. They are short and every other page links to them.
  3. Move the existing documents in docs/guide/ into Section C group 6 and Section D. They are already written and cover decision trees, the knowledge base, farm management, the capability catalog and the index gap analysis, in English and Arabic.
  4. Write Section D from the seed files, one catalog at a time. Generate the long tables from the database rather than typing them.
  5. Write the How-To groups in order 1, 5, 8, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 10, 9. That order matches what a new user hits first.
  6. Decide the site generator. The marketing site already uses Astro, so the docs site can reuse it and the same palette.
  7. Add Arabic. The product is bilingual, so the site should be too, with right-to-left layout.
