# Clip scripts — Set the grid cell size, and Attach the people

Recording scripts for the last two steps of Map the farm. Not published.

Both were captured silently against the live app first, so the pages already
have a draft clip. These scripts are for the narrated versions.

---

# Set the grid cell size

- **Target length:** 6 minutes.
- **Prerequisite:** at least one imagery product turned on for the farm.
  Without it every block is skipped and the preview reports nothing.

## Take 1 — why the grid exists (0:00 to 1:00)

**On screen:** the farm map with blocks.

**Say:** "A block gives you one number. The grid tells you which corner of that
block is dragging the number down. Cell size decides how small that corner can
be, and it is set once for the farm, not per block."

## Take 2 — find the setting (1:00 to 2:00)

**Do:** click the gear on the map toolbar. Farm settings opens. Scroll past
Satellite imagery and Weather to **Zones (sub-block grid)**.

**Say:** "The section states the rule itself: cell size and sensitivity are set
for the farm, and applied to every block that has imagery."

**Callout:** the sentence under the heading.

## Take 3 — choose a size (2:00 to 3:30)

**Do:** type a cell size.

**Say:** "Smaller cells show more variation and cost more on every pass. Larger
cells are cheaper and blur the thing you are looking for. Pick the smallest area
you would actually send someone to. A cell you would never act on alone is
detail you are paying for and ignoring."

## Take 4 — preview, and read the count (3:30 to 5:00)

**Do:** click **Preview changes**. Read the result line out loud.

**Say:** "Nothing has changed yet. The preview counts the work: how many blocks
get a new grid, how many are rezoned, how many are skipped. Skipped almost
always means the block has no imagery. If the numbers are not what you expected,
the cell size or the subscription is wrong, not the preview."

**Callout:** circle the counts.

## Take 5 — sensitivity, and the reset (5:00 to 6:00)

**Say:** "Anomaly sensitivity is how far below the field average a cell has to
fall before it is flagged. Lower is more sensitive. Leave it empty to inherit
the tenant default. It takes effect on the next sweep and reprocesses nothing,
so it is cheap to tune. And if blocks have drifted apart, Reset blocks to the
inherited default puts them all back on the farm setting."

**Close:** "Changing cell size redraws the zones, but scenes already processed
keep their old ones until they are reprocessed. That is why it asks you to
confirm and tells you the cost first."

# Attach the people

- **Target length:** 7 minutes.
- **Warning for the recording:** do not send a real invite on camera unless the
  address is yours. The welcome email is real.

## Take 1 — two screens, not one (0:00 to 0:45)

**Say:** "Adding someone takes two steps on two screens. First they join the
account and get a role across it. Then they get a role on each farm they work
on. Miss the second step and they sign in to an empty farm list."

## Take 2 — invite (0:45 to 3:00)

**Do:** Settings → Team. Show **Team & roles**. Click **Invite user**. Fill
email, full name, phone, tenant role. Send.

**Say:** "Phone is optional here, but anyone who will use the Scout app needs
one. Spell the name the way they spell it, it shows all over the app."

**Say, after sending:** "They get a welcome email. If mail is unavailable the
screen shows a one-time temporary password instead, and you hand it over
yourself. Either way they set their own password at first sign-in."

## Take 3 — give them the farm (3:00 to 5:30)

**Do:** open the farm, gear, **Members** tab. Pick the role, click Assign.

**Say:** "Five roles on the farm: Farm Manager, Agronomist, Field Operator,
Scout, Viewer. Give the smallest role that lets someone do their job."

**Be honest on camera:** "This box wants a membership ID, and the Team page does
not show one yet. For now ask your platform operator for it. We are replacing
the box with a name picker."

## Take 4 — check from their side (5:30 to 7:00)

**Do:** sign in as the new person in a private window. Show the farm picker.

**Say:** "The check that matters is not that the row appeared. It is that they
sign in and see this farm, and nothing they should not see."
